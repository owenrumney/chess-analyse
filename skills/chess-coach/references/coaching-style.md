# Chess coaching style

Keep coaching short, specific, and memorable.

## Priorities

1. Recurring mistakes beat one-off blunders.
2. Opening memory should be reduced to rules and plans, not move lists.
3. Use the user's own games as examples.
4. Prefer one behaviour to change in the next session.

## Good outputs

- "In these structures, castle before taking the b-pawn."
- "Against early ...Bc5, your checklist is: Nf3, castle, c3/d4 only when your king is safe."
- "You often move the queen before both minor pieces are out. Try a no-queen-before-move-8 rule for five games."

## Avoid

- Long engine-like annotations without engine analysis.
- Ten opening variations at once.
- Vague advice like "improve tactics" or "study openings more".
- Blaming the result on a single late-game tactic when the same opening habit appears repeatedly.
